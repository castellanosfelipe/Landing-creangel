DEVELOPING JAVA JUNIOR

We are a company dedicated to data analytics, we like what we do; that is why we have been doing it for more than 12 years. We take information from sources, types, structured data (database, proprietary applications, among others); unstructured data (text files, such as MS-Word or PDF, among others).

We are the analytical and information search company with the most experience in the national market, solving problems of search and analytics of large volumes of information using latest technology tools in Big-Data and natural language processing (NLP).

You are:

Professional in engineering, mathematics or related careers, who wants to work on these topics, who likes teamwork, where his skills can be enriched with the experience of our team and make a difference to solve problems of our clients; he has communication skills and dealing with people; complex problems, challenges inspire him to new alternatives of possible solutions, thanks to his agile mind and critical thinking ability.

Work:

  • Work with a team with years of experience in end-to-end troubleshooting (from problem data, to solution presentation interface)
  • It will create and design Big-Data components for processing large volumes of information in real time, working with an experienced team, ensuring that their creations align with the management of customer technologies and requirements.
  • It will keep the organization informed of its progress at all levels.
  • You will lead with the example, using the best practices of performance testing, capacity planning, monitoring, alerts and event response.
  • It will collaborate with the research team working on multiple problems of NLP and Deep Learning in putting the different components into production.

What you need to apply:

  • Professional engineering or related careers, with professional card,
  • Web development experience of minimum 6 months (professional practice account) at JAVA
  • Management of JAVA and Python
  • Experience in Servlet creation, web session management and stateless applications

Activities to be developed:

  • Develop components in JAVA and Python
  • Assist in Product Failure (Support)
  • Create component operation manuals
  • Deliver documentation of developed components
  • Maintain meetings with the working team of the other areas of the organization
  • Create and consume REST API and Web services
  • Deploy components in containers
  • Make deployments via application servers
  • Report monthly consumption of times per customer
  • Conduct timelines that include technical activities and implementation times
  • Perform trainings and trainings for our clients
  • Technical documentation of activities carried out
