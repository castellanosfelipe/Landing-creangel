Creangel. Information that finds its place.

We develop applications to find and use the knowledge of organizations.

A history built around information.

Creangel was founded in Bogotá, Colombia, in May 2004. Our experience spans search applications for structured and unstructured information, and optical character recognition (OCR).

We integrate tools and services to find, extract, visualize, filter and share information from different business applications.

We have worked with public and private organizations on enterprise search, data analysis and access to institutional knowledge.
