IFINDIT CHATGEN

Put your organization's knowledge within reach through assistants that help your teams find information and complete tasks.

Help teams to get answers from the knowledge of the organization.

Key capabilities

A first look at what you can do with this product.

  1. 01

    Document-based responses

    Refer to documented knowledge through responses based on available sources.

  2. 02

    Agents and tools

    Accompany tasks with attendees who can consult information and use connected tools.

  3. 03

    Custom Widgets

    Adapt each conversation experience to the context and needs of your users.

  4. 04

    Conversations with context

    Keep the thread of the conversation to give continuity to each query.

  5. 05

    WhatsApp, voice and languages

    Attend interactions in more channels, by voice and in different languages.

  6. 06

    Administration and control

    Control the access, use and activity of attendees.
