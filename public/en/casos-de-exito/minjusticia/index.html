MinJusticia

PORTAL MINISTRY OF JUSTICE

The application based on the search of the portal of the Ministry of Justice has allowed the entity to improve levels of transparency and access to information, promote citizen participation, promote the entity and sector towards citizens and comply with the guidelines of Government Online.

The application has been developed for the Ministry of Justice so that citizens can easily and agilely find all public information related to the entity, so that all people can access the information on accountability of the Ministry of Justice and improve their perception of the Colombian State.

The search engine is located on the front page of the Ministry of Justice portal is easily accessible, people can find all the information they require by just writing a word that is contained in what they want to find.
