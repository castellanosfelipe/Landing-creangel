What is IFINDIT®?

It is an increasing collection of services arranged in a private cloud that using Big Data technologies allows you to read, clean transform, enrich, cross, operate, find, visualize, publish, deliver data of different types to generate value.

It comprises a set of integrated tools, pre-designed templates and managed services that make it easier to create and manage information.

It also includes core products such as advanced data analytics and intelligent search.

It uses technologies such as Machine learning, Natural Language Processing, Spark, foundational models (Generative Artificial Intelligence), columnar databases among others.

Given its maturity level, it allows for agile, efficient, cost-effective solutions to improve access to information, knowledge generation, informed decision-making, among other challenges

IFINDIT® is a platform based on a private native cloud architecture, optimized for real-time applications that combines agile elastic storage capacity, with deep search capabilities, unparalleled relevance and AI capabilities. This architecture allows Creangel to offer its solutions under the SAAS modality, without requiring servers on the client side, this eliminates all operating expenses related to technological infrastructure so that you can start working quickly and scale your needs with a platform based on Big Data

It is clear that most organizations have difficulty exploiting the wealth of their content and transforming it into something that is accessible and easy to use. At the same time the content grows rapidly, which makes the challenge bigger over time.

IFINDIT Information Services helps you generate knowledge from your organization's structured data.

The IFINDIT® platform is unique in its ability to access content in more than 250 types of formats in the most diverse types of information sources, including ready-to-use multiple connectors and “converters” to extract text data from information systems. More capabilities are routinely incorporated that allow for greater visibility across the organizations' digital ecosystem.

IFINDIT® uses Big Data-related techniques for agile data consumption, allowing it to incorporate around 2,000,000 documents or 10,000,000 structured data per hour into the platform.

Connectors include systems such as databases, document managers, web portals, shared folders, proprietary applications, etc.

IFINDIT® components allow you to detect and clean text that may even be contained in scanned documents such as images and return data as results a set of entities of many categories. In some of them, relationships between entities can even be generated. These relationships allow for better understanding of the context when an entity refers to several subjects.

IFINDIT® has been developed and specially trained to work in Spanish, this is key to the extent that even in documents of a highly formal level there are terms of local scope, but there is an important volume of texts corresponding to natural language that are also subject to analysis for this reason working with specific language becomes even more important. This factor coupled with an intelligent text extraction that uses custom dictionaries, text cleaning algorithms and artificial intelligence to improve the accuracy of the base text makes the accuracy, quality and quantity of identified data greater than in other tools of general scope.

Once the text is available, IFINDIT® allows you to clean the data, “interpret” the different types of content fed to the platform. To do this, it combines natural language processing technologies, dictionaries, artificial intelligence, among other techniques that allow noise removal, produce cleaner data, enrich information. All of this serves as a basis for providing accurate analytics, relevant searches and strengthening machine learning.

IFINDIT® Uses machine learning that is essential to transform data into relevant information and know-how. The platform learns from examples that allow training of models already incorporated to meet specific information needs such as those related in the table below or for specific requirements (on demand)

All data generated from the analyses are subject to classifications, clusters and other operations that allow for the conversion of data into search filters, some of the data recognition and extraction capabilities are:

  • Identification of person-type entities
  • Detection of Artists, Politicians, Writers, Science Fiction Characters in ENGLISH
  • Association between roles, professions, jobs and types of work in SPANISH
  • Detection of geographic locations, cities, countries, departments, man-made structures – including local ones
  • Detection of governmental, commercial, service, non-profit, medical, sports, stock exchange organizations including local
  • Detection of local festivals and cultural events, sporting events, popular events
  • Detection of international products and LOCALS
  • Skills detection, physical addresses, phone numbers, emails, IP addresses
  • Date detection
  • Detection of numerical quantities
  • Classification by type of similar document

Technical conditions for the use of software

Since IFINDIT® is a platform based on a private native cloud architecture, you will NOT require client-side servers to benefit from the computing power offered, so to start the platform you are given access to IFINDIT® by:

(i) a user and password to the administration portal or

(ii) A connection through a VPN site to site to the sources to integrate

(iii) A connection through a VPN

IFINDIT Requires access with read-only privileges to information sources.
