THE POWER OF ENTERPRISE SEARCH: TURNING INFORMATION CHAOS INTO A COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE

Have you ever wondered how many productive hours are lost in your company looking for information?

What follows is a scene that is repeated with alarming frequency in organizations of all sizes: desperate emails, calls to different departments, unsuccessful searches in digital folders and, finally, the uncomfortable promise of “we will send you that information later”.

According to recent studies of business trends by 2025, companies that do not adopt new technologies to optimize their internal processes will find themselves at a clear competitive disadvantage. Digitization is no longer optional, it is the minimum requirement to survive in an increasingly demanding environment.

At CREANGEL, we decided to face this problem with an apparently simple but revolutionary question:

As experts in enterprise productivity point out, “when there is no clear plan or defined structure for managing information, it is easy to have performance-related problems.” IFINDIT Search provides precisely that structure, transforming chaos into clarity.
