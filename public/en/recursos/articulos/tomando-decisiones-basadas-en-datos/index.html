Making Decisions Based on Data or Assumptions?

Published at 2025-06-05 · Portal Creangel.

Editorial archive of Creangel. The references, figures and conditions correspond to the original publication.

Are you making decisions based on data or assumptions?

Imagine this scenario: it is Monday morning, and in the boardroom, each department presents its vision based on different interpretations of the data. The commercial director shows figures on paper, the marketing manager talks about “market sentiments”, and the financial director mentions “observed trends”. Meanwhile, the crucial question arises: Are we making decisions based on facts or hunches?

This situation is more common than it seems. Although 98.6% of executives aspire to a data-based culture, only 32.4% have succeeded in implementing it effectively. The difference between wanting a data-driven culture and actually having it may be costing your company millions of lost opportunities and wrong decisions.

The Gap between Data and Decisions

In today's business environment, there is a constant struggle between intuition and data. While experience is valuable, the data provide an objective basis for informed decision-making. However, many organizations still rely on assumptions due to the lack of adequate tools for effectively interpreting and visualizing information.

IFINDIT Search: Transforming Data into Clear Decisions

At CREANGEL, we understand that it is not enough to have data; it is essential to be able to interpret and visualize them in a way that drives strategic decisions. That's why we developed IFINDIT Search, a solution that converts scattered information into understandable and actionable visualizations.
